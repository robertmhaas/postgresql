ProvenanceIndex Progress
========================

NB: We eventually want to fix places that use BOOTSTRAP_SUPERUSERID instead of a
real object owner by piggybacking on an existing catalog lookup. However, this
will probably require changes to lsyscache.c that may take us too far afield from
our immediate goals. So for now we are fixing these if there's an easy way to do
so but are not stressing too much about leaving them as BOOTSTRAP_SUPERUSERID if
there's no straightforward alternative.

Aggref: Looks complete. Every call site either assigns a provenance index
explicitly or is created by a memcpy of an existing node that does not need
to be updated.

WindowFunc: Looks complete. Interestingly, every call site seems to assign 0,
which raises the question of whether this node type truly needs a provenance
index in the first place (though ripping it out seems rather short-sighted, at
the same time).

FuncExpr: Looks complete insofar as direct callers of makeNode(FuncExpr) are
concerned, but there are also many indirect callers via makeFuncExpr.
build_coercion_expression needs work. get_qual_for_hash has a note indicating
that something is wrong. Other callers look superficially OK, though perhaps
bugs are lurking.

OpExpr: Here again, direct callers of makeNode(OpExpr) look OK, but there is
also make_opclause. In initsplan.c, process_implied_equality and
build_implied_join_equality lack proper handling. Other trouble spots:
operator_predicate_proof, make_partition_op_expr, match_clause_to_partition_key,
build_bound_expr (range types).

DistinctExpr, NullIfExpr: These are variants of OpExpr that are often created
by creating OpExpr and setting the tag. There is one direct makeNode(DistinctExpr)
which is already handled.

ScalarArrayOpExpr: make_partition_op_expr is a problem here just as it is for
OpExpr.

CoerceViaIO: build_coercion_expression needs work here just as it does for
FuncExpr, and get_cast_hashentry also needs work.

RowCompareExpr: Looks complete. Only two call sites: one is a direct parser
input, and the other attempts to construct a proper array of indexes.

MinMaxExpr: Not handled. Doesn't seem like there is much going on here.

JsonExpr: Not handled. Also doesn't seem like there is much going on here.
